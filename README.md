A simple web-based text editor that lets you write content and share it instantly via a compressed URL—no database, no backend, nothing to save. Your content lives entirely in the URL hash.

# Features

Live Writing – Type directly into the editor.

Instant URL Sharing – Click the Share button to copy a URL containing your content.

No Backend Required – Everything runs in the browser. Your content is compressed and stored in the URL.

Persistent via URL – Open the shared URL, and your content is automatically loaded.

Clean Interface – Minimal black-and-white editor with a subtle info footer.

#  How It Works

Type in the editor.

The content is automatically compressed using pako and encoded into the URL hash.

Click Share to copy the URL to your clipboard.

Anyone opening that URL will see your content loaded instantly.
